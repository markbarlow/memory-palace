A song from Andy's and my band Cuzco came top of a spurious BT Online chart, and I spent the cash prize on The Magnetic Fields' triple CD [*69 Love Songs*](https://open.spotify.com/album/0K5FvRzJl6iTKikI9tMdAB?si=KPG2Mmm6TJikcl923u7fTw). At first the musical variety made the strongest impression. I particularly liked the pared-down simplicity of *Come Back from San Francisco*, the 80s synths of *I Can't Touch You Anymore*, and the laconic country duet *Papa Was a Rodeo*. The singalong chorus of the musical theatre number *Busby Berkeley Dreams* caught my attention too. 

The first lyric I remember standing out was the sardonic wit of *I Don't Want to Get Over You*: 

*I could make a career of being blue*  
*I could dress in black and read Camus*  
*Smoke clove cigarettes and drink vermouth*  
*Like I was 17, that would be a scream*  
*But I don't wanna get over you* 

Here was some pretentious wallowing I could definitely recognise. Talking of which, at some point I decided I should give the triple effort its due and listen to it from start to finish lying on the floor in my room under the eaves. That was when the extraordinary invention and tremendous number of different ideas in the lyrics astounded me. On that occasion as the minutes passed the music faded into the background and I found myself listening to the words ever more closely.

In November 2023 I decided to do a long run from North London to Richmond. The main aim was to take in the river path I'd previously only travelled in shorter sections near Westminster and Kingston. I also planned to finish up in Richmond Park where I'd never run before. Technically this distance would count as my first ultra marathon, and only one to this date, though the 50km threshold does feel a bit of a cheat being only a few kilometres further than a standard marathon. A robust, hefty 100km is much more worthy of the ultra title, and I've never tried that. 

I listened to a podcast to get me down to the houses of parliament, then as soon as I hit the riverside pressed play on *69 Love Songs* for my second uninterrupted triple album marathon. 2 hours 52 minutes of musical transportation along the meandering river and up to the deer in the park.
