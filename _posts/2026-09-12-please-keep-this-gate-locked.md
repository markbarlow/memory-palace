I have never once come across this gate closed. I pass it regularly when running through Alexandra Palace park on the way to Finsbury Park, my standard run-train commute combination. 

At the time of writing, it is indeed [open on streetview](https://www.google.com/maps/@51.5971053,-0.1211485,48a,37.5y,141.37h,84.37t/data=!3m7!1e1!3m5!1sfTvYxRgoE_D7bGvOJFiDcA!2e0!6shttps:%2F%2Fstreetviewpixels-pa.googleapis.com%2Fv1%2Fthumbnail%3Fcb_client%3Dmaps_sv.tactile%26w%3D900%26h%3D600%26pitch%3D5.63014626331335%26panoid%3DfTvYxRgoE_D7bGvOJFiDcA%26yaw%3D141.37222905544462!7i16384!8i8192?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDgzMS4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D) too:
![Open gate captured on Google Streetview](/memory-palace/assets/images/posts/blog/2026/gate-unlocked-streetview.png)


More proof, if ever needed, that people do not heed instructions. Please close to the tune of [People Ain't No Good](https://open.spotify.com/track/7gXDZrCMJ0jV9yAf4hZ0Z3?si=6063a35afb194b1e).
